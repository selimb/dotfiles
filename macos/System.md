## Keyboard Shortcuts

- **Windows**
    - **General**
        - **Fill*: Cmd+Shift+M
    - **Halves**
        - **Tile Left Half**: Cmd+Shift+Left
        - **Tile Right Half**: Cmd+Shift+Right
        - **Tile Top Half**: Cmd+Shift+Up
        - **Tile Bottom Half**: Cmd+Shift+Down
